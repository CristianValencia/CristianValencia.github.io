Wider screens (Claude Design canvas, "Home — desktop"): the social icons in a white pill in the centre, and the cycling line in a pill near the band's bottom. The icons are gray until the cursor comes into the footer, then fade to their brand colors with the footer's light. Hovering one fades the line into that account's handle. The gold bar holds the copyright, centered, and Back to top on the right. Phones get a compact version: a shorter band with smaller pills (no handles, since there's no hover), and the same gold bar. {%- endcomment -%}
