## Reference review (with Claude)

### 1. `color-palette.png`
Colors: #09191E (dark petrol), #9F6E32, #B98946, #C7A060 (golds), #F5EFE0, #F8F3E7, #FAF8F0, #FAFAFA (creams).
- What I like: everything. The golds evoke **warmth**, one of the most incredible human feelings. The many light, not-pure-white tones evoke **calm, space, tranquility, something natural**.
- Use: **cream background, gold as accent**, dark petrol for text.

### 2. `bento-home.jpg`
- Take: **card grid** (whole site on one screen, cards of different sizes) and **pill labels** (small pill on top, big title + subtitle at the bottom).
- Not taken: the single loud card, the status/quick-facts cards.

### 3. `13254295_…_n.jpg` (watercolor fox)
- Take: **hand-made texture** (watercolor, imperfect organic strokes — made by a person) and **earthy colors on cream** (olive green, soft orange alongside the palette).
- Not taken: botanical ornaments, the Zelda/playful nostalgia.

### 4. `1minus1 [com].png`
- Take: **illustration that tells a story** — the drawing explains an idea (two worlds joining), it doesn't just decorate. Could tell the photo + code side of me.
- Not taken: ink ornaments, nav subtitles, small surprise details.

### 5. `Harry's - Great Shave. Fair Price. Simple..png`
- Take: **how secondary colors are used** (one deep main color, a muted accent used sparingly for buttons, labels and links), **spaced uppercase headings** ("OUR PRODUCTS"), **dotted-underline links** in the accent color ("LEARN MORE").
- Not taken: product-photo style, the photo + color-block row.

### 6. `andre-goncalves [com] 2010.com.png`
- Take: **first-person greeting** ("Hi, my name is… I'm a…", two tones: muted for the connecting words, dark for the key ones), **own illustrated icons** (each section/network gets a drawn character or object, not a generic logo), **one-screen simplicity** (everything fits in one view, little text, lots of air).
- Not taken: dotted separators, edge vignette.

### 7–8. `ekaterinatrukhan [com].png` and `… 2.png`
- Take: **grid gallery** (the work is the hero, an orderly grid with no text over the images — base for the photography gallery) and **thin, widely spaced uppercase menu** (WORK · ABOUT · BLOG; confirms the spaced type from Harry's).
- Not taken: hand-painted logo, the very sparse white page.

### 9. `mercado_tercera_cartel.jpg`
- Take: **engraving / ink style** (black linocut or woodcut stroke: handmade, textured, very Mexican) and **the camera at the center** (photography as the heart of everything — an idea for the storytelling illustration).
- Not taken: the local/market theme as such.

### 10. `rehoboth-map.jpg`
- Take: **its colors feel like my palette** — sand tones from brown to cream.
- Open: whether the turquoise of the sea joins the palette as a secondary color.

### 11. `roybarber [com].png`
- Take: **monochrome** — one background color with everything else in tints of the same color. Could be a card or section in a gold or the dark petrol.
- Not taken: warm serif, split menu around a central logo, the personal paragraph.

### 12. `simonfosterdesign [com] 3.png`
- Take: **typography for long reading** and **comfortable reading** (narrow centered column, large serif text, generous line height) — the model for blog posts. **Minimal header** (just name and three links; nothing distracts from the text).
- Not taken: Q&A format, recent posts + categories footer.

### 13. `top-0e02f3daab8bc24bbacce40499928333.jpg` (photo book)
- Take: **warm light and wood** (afternoon light, amber tones — the palette as a photo) and **windows onto the photos** (die-cut cover peeking at several photos — idea for the Photography card: a mosaic that peeks through).
- Not taken: physical/printed feel, dark-on-warm contrast.
