--- layout: default title: Photography header: static --- {%- comment -%} Photography: the photos in a cascade (VSCO-style). Each photo keeps its own proportions, nothing is cropped, and the columns fill side by side: two on phones, three on wider screens. The header stays at the top (header: static, as on Contact), and a row of buttons picks which photos show: Curated (the default), All, Black & White, Color. The photos come from _data/photos.yml. cascade.js does the filtering and places each photo in the shortest column, so the order runs across the page; without it, the buttons stay hidden and every photo shows, in columns read top to bottom. {%- endcomment -%}
