Each key matches # the subjects on the photos in _data/photos.yml. subjects: - key: street label: Street - key: people label: People - key: nature label: Nature - key: travel label: Travel --- {%- comment -%} Photography: the photos in a cascade (VSCO-style). Each photo keeps its own proportions, nothing is cropped, and the columns fill side by side: two on phones, three on wider screens. The header stays at the top (header: static, as on Contact). Two controls pick which photos show, side by side (one per line on phones), each a picker (the current choice with a chevron) that opens a floating menu with all the options: - Category, the subject: Curated (the default), All, Street, People, Nature or Travel, one at a time; - Color, the tone: All (the default; both tones), Chrome (color photos) or Monochrome (black and white), on top of any subject. Chrome and Monochrome match the Instagram accounts (cris.chrome, cris.monochrome); in the data and the link they stay color and bw. The choice is kept in the link (?subject=street&tone=bw), so a view can be shared and Back returns to it. Choices with no photos are greyed out. The photos come from _data/photos.yml. cascade.js does the filtering and places each photo in the shortest column, so the order runs across the page; without it, the controls stay hidden and every photo shows, in columns read top to bottom. {%- endcomment -%}
