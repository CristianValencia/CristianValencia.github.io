The landing (/photography/) links here, to Curated by default or to a filtered view. Each photo keeps its own proportions, nothing is cropped, and the columns fill side by side: two on phones, three on wider screens. The header stays at the top (header: static, as on Contact). Two controls pick which photos show, side by side (one per line on phones), each a picker (the current choice with a chevron) that opens a floating menu with all the options: - Category, the subject: Curated (the default), All, Street, People, Nature or Travel, one at a time; - Tone: All (the default; both tones), Color or Monochrome (black and white), on top of any subject. In the data and the link they're color and bw. Monochrome turns the page black and white: no gold anywhere, and the header in the black-and-white glass. The choice is kept in the link (?subject=street&tone=bw), so a view can be shared and Back returns to it. Choices with no photos are greyed out. The photos come from _data/photos.yml. cascade.js does the filtering and places each photo in the shortest column, so the order runs across the page; without it, the controls stay hidden and every photo shows, in columns read top to bottom. {%- endcomment -%} {%- comment %} A Monochrome link opens black and white from the first paint: no gold (cascade.js keeps it in step as the filters change). {% endcomment %}
